Our services are billed on a recurring monthly retainer basis. By providing your payment information, you authorize Venith Emmerce LLC to charge your designated payment method automatically at the beginning of each billing cycle for the fees associated with your subscription plan.

3. Cancellation Policy

You, the Customer, maintain the right to cancel your subscription at any time.

  • Procedure: To cancel your service, you must submit a cancellation request via email to hello@venithemmerce.com.
  • Notice Period: We strictly require that cancellation requests be received at least two (2) business days prior to your next scheduled billing date to avoid being charged for the subsequent cycle.

We adhere to a strict "No Refund" policy for partial subscription periods.

Any one-time fees paid for "Onboarding," "Setup," "Implementation," or "Custom Development" are strictly non-refundable. These fees cover the manual labor, technical configuration, and proprietary resource allocation required to initialize your specific AI agent. Once these resources have been deployed, the cost cannot be recovered by Venith Emmerce LLC.

We will submit this policy and your usage logs to Stripe and your bank as evidence that the charge was authorized. Venith Emmerce LLC reserves the right, at its sole and absolute discretion, to consider refund requests on a case-by-case basis. Granting a refund in one instance does not create an obligation to grant refunds in future instances. Generally, refunds are only considered in the event of a persistent, critical technical failure of our platform that renders the service completely unusable for a period exceeding 72 hours, provided that such failure was not caused by third-party carriers or the Customer’s own systems.
